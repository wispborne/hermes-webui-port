# UX notes: mobile app, Nov 1, 2026

I used the mobile app the way a new user would and wrote down what I'd change.
I compared it with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Open WebUI and LibreChat, and with Slack, Telegram and Signal for the plain chat parts.

## What I tested

On the Pixel 9 Pro emulator with the dev build:

- **Signed in to your real gateway:** I looked at the session list, settings, the new-session screen and two finished sessions. I sent nothing.
- **Signed in to the demo gateway** (`mobile/scripts/demo-gateway.mjs`): sign-in, a plain reply, a slow reply with Stop, an approval, a three-part question, a failed turn, a message queued during a turn, the outbox with the gateway down, reconnecting, and another session needing input.

Not tested: private input, images and files in replies, and notifications with Keep connected on.

## Bugs

1. **Fixed in b959a2483: a queued message split the reply that's still running.** I sent a message while a slow reply was streaming. The "Queued" message appeared in the middle of that reply, and the rest of the reply kept streaming below it. A message sent during a turn now stays below the whole reply.
2. **The sign-in screen shows the old gateway's status for the new URL.** After I changed the URL, the tick and "Hermes 0.22.5" from the old gateway stayed until I left the field. The new gateway runs 0.22.4. Clear the tick and version as soon as the URL changes.

## While a turn runs

1. **Done in a86305e04: Retry replaces the failed turn. It sends the message again as new only when the gateway can't find it as the session's last. No Retry after a failed turn.** "Turn failed. The model provider answered 500" is clear, but there's no button to try again. You have to type the message again. ChatGPT and Claude both show Retry on errors. Retry is already defined in `CONTEXT.md`.
2. **Done in 39363b36a. A stopped reply isn't marked.** After Stop, the reply ends partway through, here in the middle of a table, with no "Stopped" label. Later, nobody can tell whether Hermes finished or was cut off. ChatGPT marks it. A Continue button would also help.
3. **Done: Stop now stays next to Send. Typing hid Stop.** While a turn runs, typing replaces Stop with Send. To stop the turn, you have to clear your text first. Show both buttons while a turn runs.
4. **"Thinking" shows when Hermes isn't thinking.** The spinner says "Thinking" while the reply text is being written, and while Hermes waits for you to answer an approval or question. While a thought is open, "Thinking" appears twice: once on the thought and once on the spinner. Use "Writing…" and "Waiting for you", or no label.
5. **Half-written Markdown shows raw.** While streaming, a list item showed "`max_siz" with a stray backtick until the closing one arrived. ChatGPT and Claude hide unfinished Markdown marks.
6. **A queued message has no controls.** It shows a "Queued" label and nothing else. `CONTEXT.md` says you can edit it, delete it or steer with it.

## Approvals and questions

These work well. The approval card shows the command clearly with Run, Allow this session, Always allow and Reject. The question card has Pick one, Select all that apply, an "Other" box, Back, Skip and Next.

1. **Done: the answer goes in the card, and the message box waits with "Answer the question above". Two boxes for one answer.** During a question, the card has its own text box, and the message box at the bottom also turns into an answer box. On question 3 there were two "Type your answer…" boxes, one above the other, showing the same text. Keep the one in the card and hide or disable the bottom one.
2. **Done with the item above. The message box changed between questions.** On question 1 it said "Type your answer…" with Send. On question 2 it said "Send follow-up" with Stop. On question 3 it went back to "Type your answer…".
3. **Your choice isn't recorded.** After an approval, the transcript shows "Ran command" but not whether you chose Run, Allow this session or Always allow. After a question, your answers aren't shown. You only see them if Hermes repeats them. Claude Code leaves a short line such as "You allowed this once".

## Session list

1. **Done: they show the job name with a clock, and the message without Markdown marks. Scheduled-job sessions had raw titles.** On your real gateway they show as `[Cron delivery: USC TriOS/Wisp daily digest] **USC digest — …`, with the Markdown `**` visible. Show the job name ("USC daily digest"), a small clock icon or "Scheduled" tag, and strip the Markdown.
2. **Done with item 1. Those rows were misaligned.** They have no preview line, so the title sits lower than the time on the right.
3. **Done: long-press opens Rename, Pin, Archive (with Undo) and Delete, and Settings ▸ Archived Chats brings archived sessions back. No actions on a session.** Long-pressing a row opens it, the same as a tap, and swiping does nothing. Every chat app I compared lets you rename, delete, pin and archive from the list. Telegram and Signal also offer mute and mark as unread.
4. **Done: sessions that need input now sit at the top. Needs input is easy to miss.** When another session waits on an approval, its row gets a small red "?" after 10 to 15 seconds. The row stays where it is, so on a long list you won't see it. Move those rows to the top, or show a bar such as "1 session needs you".
5. **No date groups.** ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini group the list under Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days and older.
6. **Done with item 3. No pinned sessions.**
7. **Done: a Hide scheduled chip, remembered on the phone. No way to filter out scheduled-job sessions.** On your real gateway, cron deliveries are about a third of the visible list.
8. **Done: a preview that repeats the heading is left out. A new session repeated itself.** Until it gets a title, a new session shows its first message as both the title and the preview.
9. **The Memory review icon isn't clear.** A lightbulb with a "5" badge doesn't say "5 memories to review". A label or a different icon would help.
10. **No profiles or projects.** The web UI has projects and profiles. The phone shows one flat list.

## New session

1. **The screen is blank.** There's no greeting, no suggestions, and no choice of model, profile or working folder. ChatGPT and Claude show a model picker and a few suggested prompts. Coding agents on mobile (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) ask which repo or folder to work in.
2. **No slash commands.** Typing `/` shows nothing. The desktop has slash commands through `command.dispatch`, and Hermes users know them from the CLI (`/model`, `/memory` and others).
3. **No attach button.** You can't send a photo, take a picture or attach a file. Every AI chat app has this, and the phone is where most photos are. The `chat-images` change covers receiving images, not sending them.
4. **No voice button.** The keyboard's mic works, but ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude have their own dictation button, and most have a voice mode.
5. **The draft isn't kept.** I typed, left New session and came back, and the text was gone. ChatGPT and Slack keep a draft for each chat.
6. **Two different placeholders.** It says "Message" in a new session and "Send follow-up" in an existing one.

## Reading a session

What works: tables, highlighted code blocks with a Copy button, and compact tool rows. Long-pressing reply text starts normal text selection with Copy, Copy as Markdown, Share and Select all.

1. **No one-tap actions under a reply.** Copying a whole reply means selecting it by hand. ChatGPT and Claude show Copy and Retry under every reply.
2. **You can't edit and resend your own message.** ChatGPT and Claude both allow it.
3. **No header menu.** The header shows only the title. Other apps put rename, delete, share or export there, along with the model, profile and working folder.
4. **No information about the turn.** There are no timestamps, no turn duration, no model name and no token or cost count. Claude Code, Cursor and Codex show at least the model and usage.
5. **Opened tool calls get cut off.** The arguments and result boxes cut off long lines at the right edge, and the result is raw JSON (`{"total_count": 139, "matches_format": …`). Wrap long lines, and show the result as text when the tool returns text.
6. **Inline code is heavy.** Inline code is blue monospace on a grey background. In a summary with a dozen inline code spans, the paragraph turns into a patchwork. Claude and ChatGPT use the normal text color with a light background.
7. **No search inside a session,** and no export to Markdown or PDF.

## Connection and sign-in

What works: with the gateway down, a banner says so and has Retry. A message sent then is faded and marked "Waiting to send", with Cancel. When the gateway came back, the app reconnected, sent it, and the reply arrived without me doing anything.

1. **Sign out doesn't ask first.** It signs out at once and forgets the saved password. Ask "Sign out? You'll need your password to sign in again."
2. **The offline banner always asks about Tailscale.** "Can't reach the gateway. Is Tailscale connected?" is wrong for a gateway on a public address or on the same network. Mention Tailscale only when the URL is a Tailscale address (`*.ts.net` or `100.x.x.x`).

## Settings

1. **Settings has far less than the web UI.** There are five pages: Appearance, Memory & Context, Notifications, Gateways and About. The web UI also has models and providers, profiles, scheduled jobs, skills and tools, MCP servers, and messaging bots. Your parity goal covers these. Model choice and scheduled jobs are probably the most-wanted on a phone.
2. **Only one gateway.** Switching gateways means signing out, which also forgets the saved password. The Open WebUI and Home Assistant apps keep a list of servers and switch between them.
3. **Notifications are one switch.** "Keep connected" is the only choice. People will want separate choices for a finished reply, a turn that needs input, a failed turn and a scheduled-job delivery.
4. **About is bare.** It has no release notes, update check, log export or "send feedback".

## Dev build only

- The Expo "Tools" button floats over the Settings gear on every screen. Tapping the middle of the gear opens the dev tools instead of Settings. Moving the dev button lower would make testing easier.

## What's already better than most chat apps

- The one-line tool and thinking rows are compact and easy to scan, close to Claude Code.
- Approvals, questions and private input make it an agent client, not only a chat client. ChatGPT and Claude have nothing like Memory review.
- The outbox works: a message sent while the gateway is down waits, then goes out by itself when it's back. Most AI chat apps lose it.
- Theme and accent choices are more than most apps offer.
